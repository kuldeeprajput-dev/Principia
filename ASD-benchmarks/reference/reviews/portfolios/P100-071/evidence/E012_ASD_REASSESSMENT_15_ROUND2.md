# Reassessment of all fifteen ASD portfolios after the second continuation

30 September 2026. Original results, the 43-investigation continuation and the subsequent 33 scientific tests are assessed together.

**Case 27 remains the best balanced positive-result example. Case 61 is now the best example of iterative mechanistic model development. Case 53 remains the best example of handling realistic industrial data.** For teaching future agents to propose a physical interpretation and actively challenge it with another measured observable, case 60 is especially valuable.

These recommendations concern the success and usefulness of the investigations. **None of the fifteen portfolios currently establishes an independently confirmed new physical law or demonstrates an industrial intervention benefit.** Several contain useful equations, strong local references and informative negative evidence. Those contributions are assessed rather than erased by the absence of a universal discovery.

## Assessment method and evidence maturity

Three separate review agents inspected five portfolios each. Their documentary critiques were reconciled with direct inspection and a new, separate arithmetic audit. All **80 original saved model errors** and **180 latest exact-cohort comparison entries**, including 48 newly fitted model sets, reproduce; the largest latest difference is below 10^-12. These are score entries, not 260 distinct discoveries or independent experiments. No models were fitted, reference candidates changed or historical confirmation observations reopened for this assessment.

Both frozen continuation editions pass preservation checks: 2,011 files from the first and 525 from the second. The original source/reference preservation inventory also remains intact. Detailed evidence, grades and checks are in [the assessment support folder](_support/reassessment_round2_20260930/README.md).

Evidence has three distinct levels:

1. **Original reserved comparisons:** support only the original frozen model and its declared scope. The original case-72 qPCR schema audit had target exposure, so that result is provisional.
2. **First-continuation exposed transfer:** informative retrospective diagnostics; they cannot restore independent confirmation.
3. **Second-continuation development results:** useful whole-group or forward comparisons, with repeated model selection and no fresh experimental confirmation.

The reviews are model-assisted computational/documentary critiques, not blinded external peer review, human expert adjudication or independent experimental replication. Current literature checks are bounded. They support known-family limits and disclose access gaps; they do not certify the priority of an exact equation.

## Ratings and difficulty

I assess novelty, achieved significance, contribution, impact and process quality separately. The result grade describes supported positive/reference yield: **A** requires independently confirmed important new knowledge; **B** denotes a useful scoped extension or operational reference; **C** denotes reproduction, limited comparison or provisional yield; **D** applies to a specified unsupported mechanism. No result receives A. A B development candidate still requires fresh confirmation.

The process grade describes the quality of the investigation: **A** is a leading workflow example, **B** a sound reusable investigation with limitations, and **C** an avoidable weakness materially reducing confidence. A strong process can produce a C result when its controls correctly reject the proposed law. Plus/minus marks are qualitative judgments.

Difficulty credit is earned through well-handled heterogeneity, timing, missingness, sparse independent groups or identifiability. It is limited to one adjacent process-grade step. It does not relax source reliability, leakage safeguards, fair comparison, novelty or confirmation. Predicting a calibrated system from early measurements is also narrower than identifying a general mechanism or predicting an unseen system. Comparable agent-hour, token and compute accounts are unavailable, so this is not an economic efficiency ranking.

## Individual assessments

### 1. Case 27 - Best balanced positive-result example

**Novelty:** a scoped empirical closure built on established phase/slip descriptions. **Significance:** measured void profiles are relevant to thermal-hydraulic model validation. **Contribution:** a compact original correction with the clearest positive reserved comparison in this collection.

Original reserved MAE is **0.106701**, versus drift flux **0.139195**, a **23.34%** reduction with wins on two of three runs. The large counterexample remains: Run 32 gives **0.187872** versus drift **0.051245**. The first continuation improves its physical development error to **0.084041**. The latest slip/profile revisions fail, and a new matched flexible model scores **0.075914** on that development cohort. The physical predictor therefore is not the best available current development predictor.

Parameter variation, low-quality behavior, coordinate interpretation and sparse runs constrain a general closure claim. The source supplies profiles for computational-code validation; no intervention or safety margin was tested. [KTH source](https://zenodo.org/records/14627088).

**Impact and example value:** useful facility-conditioned validation. Copy its hypothesis, matched comparisons and visible failure regime; do not present a universal phase-distribution law. [Original evidence](27_fluid_dynamics_kth_boiling/final_results/FINDINGS.md); [first continuation](27_fluid_dynamics_kth_boiling/research_history/continuation-20260930/FINDINGS.md); [latest tests](27_fluid_dynamics_kth_boiling/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md).

### 2. Case 61 - Best iterative mechanistic-model example

**Novelty:** a local model simplification and approximation, not a new transport family. **Significance:** calibrated hydrogen-flux prediction is an important separator-modeling endpoint. **Contribution:** the strongest sequence from a concrete approximation to competing explanations, simplification and numerical falsification.

The selected developing-film closure reduces development MAE **0.004109 to 0.003340 mol m^-2 s^-1**, an **18.71%** reduction; five of six gas-temperature blocks improve. Six mixture coefficients become two, while **twelve pure-gas calibration coefficients remain**. He at 350 degrees C worsens slightly. Gas-sign and one-family controls lose. Mesh refinement preserving the local film field changes error from **0.00333994 at 64 cells to 0.00333728 at 1,024 cells**, without refitting.

That progress does not inherit the old reserve: the original compact model lost decisively to its nested kernel, and the first revised physical model still lost to that kernel on the exposed gas groups. The exact source already includes film transport, axial depletion and Sherwood modeling. Its full layered model has not been reproduced as an identical matched comparator. The reduced-mass factor also omits collision-integral and viscosity differences. [Source-matching paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2024.05.225).

**Impact and example value:** useful conservative simulation infrastructure; no improved separator or energy saving was measured. This is my first choice for teaching agents how to refine an interpretable physical model. It needs independent membrane/operating campaigns before stronger admission. [Latest findings](61_chemistry_membrane_permeation/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md); [mesh evidence](61_chemistry_membrane_permeation/research_history/continuation-20260930b/mesh_consistency/RESULT.json).

### 3. Case 53 - Best realistic industrial-data workflow

**Novelty:** local history compression and rejected wear explanations. **Significance:** early thread-forming torque prediction directly concerns manufacturing monitoring. **Contribution:** particularly strong handling of the information available at the prediction trigger.

The original memory model scores **0.023164 Nm**, versus robust linear **0.022803** and flexible **0.022296**, on fifty complete reserved workpieces. Both screw locations and all reuse cycles remain linked. Native-timing bounds are explicitly audited for history access; absolute timestamp semantics remain qualified. The latest multiplicative-wear rule scores **0.026526** versus the prior flexible **0.022947 Nm** in development and wins only **15/200 workpieces**.

This is calibrated, history-assisted monitoring. Joint strength, clamp force and scrap consequences are not measured. The separate natural-degradation S01 campaign remains inaccessible locally and provides no completed confirmation. The author distinguishes that campaign from surface-friction data. [PyScrew source paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11925).

**Impact and example value:** excellent timing/grouping/strong-control template, even with no admitted new wear law. Do not turn a controller decision or reused torque measurement into an independent physical target. [Investigation and audit](53_manufacturing_screw_friction/research_history/continuation-20260930/FINDINGS.md); [latest negative test](53_manufacturing_screw_friction/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md).

### 4. Case 72 - Best new practical endpoint

**Novelty:** a compact conditional sugar forecast; exact formula priority remains unresolved. Differential glucose/fructose behavior and consumption models are established. **Significance:** later residual sugar is a concrete fermentation endpoint. **Contribution:** finding a separately measured task with a compatible input budget and interpretable competing clocks.

The original qPCR result remains provisional because its schema audit exposed targets. It is distinct from the HPLC task. The latter uses each fermentation's 22/72 h assays, holds out whole strains and links technical/biological representations. Two substrate clocks reduce development MAE **14.656974 to 13.464268 g/L**, **8.14%**, with five of six strains improving; Ec1118 worsens. A context parameter gains less than 1%, so the simpler rule is retained. A matched flexible model scores **14.562860**.

Two exposed transfer strains from the preceding continuation are not fresh confirmation for the new clocks. Effective depletion exponents do not identify biomass, transporter or nutrient mechanisms. Prior primary experiments already model glucose/fructose consumption. [Prior consumption-model study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8160661/).

**Impact and example value:** promising early-assay planning, without measured fermentation-control benefit. Copy the endpoint audit, compatible evaluator, competing explanations and simplicity decision. [Latest findings](72_biotechnology_yeast_competition/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md).

### 5. Case 60 - Best prediction-versus-mechanism test

**Novelty:** a local excitation-response formulation in an established oscillator family. **Significance:** apparatus-specific peak calibration. **Contribution:** strong predictive improvement coupled to an explicit, independently measured contradiction of its physical interpretation.

The shared-Q, two-edge response reduces normalized development MAE **0.155761 to 0.100101**, **35.73%**. Physical voltage MAE falls **0.536629 to 0.258478 V**. Twenty of thirty files improve and all five width means improve. However, contact at nominal 500 kHz worsens across all five widths: its mean normalized error rises **0.05643 to 0.18303**. Original confirmation contained just one reserved width, not new sensors.

For the selected cycle-004, native spectra agree within 5% of the effective fitted frequency for only **7/15** nominal 110 kHz files and **0/15** nominal 500 kHz files. The spectral record's initial RESULT refers to cycle-002; the [selected-model comparison](60_acoustics_ultrasonic_transmission/research_history/continuation-20260930b/waveform_falsification/selected_model_comparison.csv) is the correct anchor here. No resonance shift or time-base error is identified.

**Impact and example value:** useful pulse-width prediction within this apparatus; no unseen-device transfer or hardware improvement. Copy the practice of challenging a plausible named parameter with another observable, preserving both success and contradiction. [Latest findings](60_acoustics_ultrasonic_transmission/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md).

### 6. Case 71 - Strong difficult-biology controls

**Novelty:** local sensor-history correction, with established phase effects. **Significance:** online viable-cell-density prediction. **Contribution:** causal sensor alignment, quality/missingness handling and careful comparison with availability and spectral controls.

The first continuation improves the earlier exposed reference by **22.70%**, but its apparent advantage over the flexible control wins only one of three exposed experiment pairs. The strongest development linear spectral control remains better. The latest optical/electrical moment closure scores **2.022590** versus **1.793650 million cells/mL**, winning only two of nine development pairs.

Cell size, viability, membrane properties and sensor availability remain confounded. Difficulty credit is justified for handling those complications, not for claiming new cell physiology. Offline references are targets, not hidden online predictors.

**Impact and example value:** potential calibration/monitoring use without demonstrated assay replacement or culture-yield improvement. A good biological template for testing whether a mechanistic effect is really sensor availability or phase history. ### 8. Case 73 - Competing kinetics and non-identifiability

**Novelty:** known accumulation/pool families, with no unique biological pool identified. **Significance:** later in-vitro total-gas prediction. **Contribution:** demonstrating that interpretable-looking curve decompositions need not be identified or strongest.

Original run-RMSE **7.501 mL** loses tuned kernel **7.243**. The latest fractional curve scores **29.862** versus kernel **7.615 mL** and loses all nine scoring blocks. ### 9. Case 67 - Best honest falsification lesson

**Novelty:** the proposed orientation/interface extension is not supported; the useful contribution is negative evidence. **Significance:** transit across measured fluid configurations. **Contribution:** careful semantics repair and clear defeat of an attractive mechanistic correction by an elementary control.

Original parameter-free upstream-speed transit gives absolute log error **0.117418**, versus orientation **0.366041**, and wins every reserved configuration. Latest entry-velocity blending loses the development RBF on five of six configurations. Two-dimensional orientation, unresolved geometry and absent normal stresses limit mechanism identification.

**Impact and example value:** scoped transport testing, without demonstrated particle separation or remediation benefit. Difficulty earns process credit for appropriate abstention. ### 10. Case 82 - Small, credible history refinement

**Novelty:** reproduction and a local moisture-history refinement consistent with known rewetting effects. **Significance:** calibrated-context respiration prediction. **Contribution:** repaired numerical fitting and directional controls, rather than discovery of new ecology.

Latest mean forward-block RMSE falls **0.240895 to 0.237048 g CO2 m^-2 h^-1**, **1.60%**, winning 28/48 blocks. Twelve of sixteen source-label mean effects are positive. Labels can represent related locations/treatments and are not sixteen independent sites. Drying-only and absolute-change controls lose, while reoptimizing the no-pulse counterpart also makes a small gain; not all improvement is attributable to wetting.

Substrate, season, sampling intervals and calibrated context amplitudes remain confounded. Moisture history is not a rainfall or microbial intervention measurement.

**Impact and example value:** exploratory temporal covariate, without carbon-management benefit demonstrated. Copy chronology, countermodels and modest claims. [Latest findings](82_ecology_soil_respiration/research_history/continuation-20260930b/FINDINGS.md).

## What changed and what to imitate

Compared with the previous assessment, **61 rises from 3 to 2**, **60 from 8 to 5**, and **82 from 13 to 10**. These reflect substantive model discrimination, better predictions and repaired evidence. They do not mean fresh confirmation was obtained. Case 27 remains first for the best balanced positive result; case 53's move from 2 to 3 reflects case 61's improvement, not a loss of its timing/grouping strengths. Exact movements within close tiers should not be overinterpreted.

For future agents, use **61** for physical reconstruction, competing mechanisms, coefficient reduction and numerical consistency; **27** for a scoped positive equation with reserved evidence and an explicit failing regime; **53** for causal input budgets, linked industrial units and strong comparators; **60** for measured-observable falsification; and **67/100** for recognizing when a simpler control or a negative outcome is the real contribution.

Before promoting any latest candidate, obtain genuinely fresh groups with models/calibration/selection frozen in advance. Literature review must evaluate the exact proposed advance, including the missing full author-model comparison in case 61. A benchmark should admit alternative valid equations and justified abstention, not force agreement with unsupported mechanistic interpretations.
