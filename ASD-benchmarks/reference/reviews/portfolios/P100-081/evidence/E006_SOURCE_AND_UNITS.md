# Source and units

DigitAF eight-country soil measurements, authoritative Zenodo record 21204617 and country-specific metadata sheets. Targets are the original workbook bulk density column (g/cm³), carbon is organic carbon mass percent, pH is measured in water, and depth is the midpoint of each explicit sampled interval in cm. Repeated depth/transect records remain in the same country partition.

Missing values are not imputed. Texture exists mainly for Finland and the Netherlands; it is not required. Carbon stocks are not used: they are derived using bulk density and would leak the target. Two Finland rows were inspected for schema, so they cannot be confirmation.
