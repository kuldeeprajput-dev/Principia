# NOAA tornado damage and geometry

TOR_LENGTH is miles and TOR_WIDTH is maximum yards, converted exactly to km. DAMAGE_PROPERTY strings K/M/B expand to USD; blank/unparsable damage is missing, never0. Endpoint ln(1+USD) covers reported zeros and compresses broad estimates. EF scale derives from damage and is forbidden; source narratives/casualties/crop loss are also forbidden. Path width is maximum, so length*width bounds a footprint proxy and cannot identify actual exposed capital. Post-event loss estimates are not independent wind-energy measurements.

Whole NOAA episode IDs keep linked segments together. Groups split before inspecting error; unspecified cross-office outbreak dependencies remain. Source primary guidance confirms broad damage estimates and maximum path width. Target eligibility/no-imputation explicit.
