# Source and units

Federal Reserve2025 SHED public2025.csv. EF3_h is the inability-to-pay400USD checkbox0/1, not the broader insufficient-cash measure; do not conflate these. Final cross-sectional weight is weight; panel weights are unused. Inputs exclude other EF3 checkboxes, maximum affordable-expense question and emergency-savings questions. ppinc7 brackets map to declared [5,17.5,37.5,62.5,87.5,125,175] thousandUSD proxies; last is open-ended. D1A0 indicates no work for pay/profit lastmonth; the internal column name shock is only a computational alias and never evidence of involuntary loss. Age/household count numeric. Complete domains and positive weights required. Whole-state hash split prevents geographic overlap but not external national transfer.

The public codebook aggregate EF3_h frequencies were seen during schema audit; held individual targets and scores were not inspected. No unique causal economic mechanism established from contemporaneous self-reports.
