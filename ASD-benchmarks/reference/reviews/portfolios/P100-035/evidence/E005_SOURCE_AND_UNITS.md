# Vocal context and grouping

3181 source-extracted calls span seven ungulate species and168 animals after canonical linkage. The WAV filename label WidlBoar occurs for10 animals also spelled WildBoar; the source workbook identifies these as Wild boar. Both spellings remain in native filenames, while grouping links the animal to prevent leakage. Different species with a coincident ID remain distinct.

The target is positive/negative source context annotation, not direct access to subjective emotional state. Context words, reference study, animal and call index cannot enter predictors. Known species is allowed for every model. Fixed summaries use resampling8kHz and normalized PCM amplitude; no physical sound-pressure calibration is available. Log RMS is therefore a recording-amplitude diagnostic, not a calibrated loudness law. Spectral peak is not declared fundamental pitch.

All calls from a held animal are excluded from fitting. Study/species/context and recorder remain confounded; animal transfer cannot prove cross-recorder or cross-context deployment. Unequal calls per animal are equalized in fitting/scoring.
