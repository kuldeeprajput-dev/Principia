# Scientific-critical reviews of the 25 explored portfolios

These reviews distinguish scoped prediction, established-model reproduction, retrospective extension, measurement audit and informative falsification. They are advisory, evidence-bound scientific assessments, not new discoveries or independent experimental replication.

All current outcomes are exposed. This includes the PLA challenge that was historically preregistered before response opening; its earlier evidential status is retained, and its future availability is explicit. Corrected BikeZ raw-position results require supplemental repository assets rather than the original core trajectories.

Each JSON binds finding-level judgments to SHA-256 evidence assets and supplies six distinct dimensions: reliability, mechanism, novelty, significance, reproducibility and impact. Statuses are supported, partially_supported, unsupported or not_assessed. There is no numerical composite, no assumption that a flexible predictor establishes a physical mechanism, and no requirement that future valid submissions duplicate the reference equation.

The reviews use retained prior-art/source records only. They do not claim an exhaustive new literature search. Independent computational replay and raw-to-prepared reconstruction belong to separate verification artifacts. Strong negative evidence is retained as a first-class finding. Scientific difficulty is expressed through measured scope, identifiability and required evidence rather than a relaxed validity standard.

`INDEX.json` lists every scenario. Each `P100-NNN.json` has a readable Markdown counterpart. Source evidence paths currently identify legacy root-relative artifacts; portable release assembly must snapshot those exact bytes and map paths without changing their hashes.
