# Prior-art and novelty assessment

The source study describes respiratory monitoring under rapid occlusion and model-based work-of-breathing analysis. Persistence, pressure-slope extrapolation and autoregressive forecasting are established controls; predictive success does not identify resistance, compliance, muscle pressure or clinical utility.

Primary sources read during this campaign: https://physionet.org/content/respiratory-heartrate-dataset/1.0.0/ and https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifacol.2023.10.1107. Source methods and known conclusions informed hypotheses before selection. Targeted title/domain searches were performed on2026-10-01. Search records in `PRIOR_ART.json` identify the primary URLs. This limited targeted review does not establish exhaustive novelty.
