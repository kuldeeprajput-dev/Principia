# P100-024 current quality audit

Targeted current audit of existing source-bound scientific evidence. It is combined with whole-collection native reconstruction, replay and independent numerical checks; it is not a new discovery attempt.

## Supported scope

Logarithmic stiffness degradation improves early-modulus persistence within the campaign.

## Limits and counterevidence

Conditional observed stiffness is not remaining useful life; survival/runout selection and the small number of specimens limit inference.



## What would improve the science

Add censoring-aware lifetime endpoints only with complete failure/runout records and new whole-specimen confirmation.
