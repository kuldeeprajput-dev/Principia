# P100-083 current quality audit

Targeted current audit of existing source-bound scientific evidence. It is combined with whole-collection native reconstruction, replay and independent numerical checks; it is not a new discovery attempt.

## Supported scope

A modest, calibrated one-hour hive-temperature forecast is supported; thermal conductance, biological setpoints and thermoregulation are not identified. Physically signed passive and weather-increment challengers fail stronger flexible controls.

## Limits and counterevidence

Five sensor streams in one apiary are not independent colonies/sites. Species, sensor placement and season are confounded.

Causal one-hour lag and target matching tolerances matter; original uses future dates while round2 is development OOF with an extra ambient lag.

Negative ambient-gradient coefficient cannot be positive passive conductance; 35.72 degC derived coefficient combination is not a measured brood setpoint.

Round2 calibration prose appends coefficients of the older reference after the training-scope description; future readers should use per-fold states, not treat those old coefficients as the round2 equation.

The original reference loses to residual kernel; continuation loses13/15 blocks. These negative outcomes deserve equal prominence.

## What would improve the science

Add independent apiaries and colonies with sensor placement and response calibration; reserve complete seasons/sites.

Obtain heat-flow or controlled perturbation evidence before interpreting conductance or active regulation.

Keep statistical forecast quality separate from biological mechanism and verify timestamp tolerances in external submissions.
