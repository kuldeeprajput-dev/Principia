# Lessons for later agents

Sparse calibration is a major information budget: disclose every endpoint. Compare against a training-only normalized template, not only poor straight-line controls. Calibration-coupled curvature transfers within this measured array, but an exponent near2 at low drain bias does not prove a saturation-law mechanism. Off-regime physical-current error is tiny, so the negative-branch exponent is weakly identified. Whole-device evidence is much stronger than random-row interpolation but does not show new-lot transfer.
