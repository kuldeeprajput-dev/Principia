# Lessons for later agents

Declare exact administrative endpoint instead of inferring status semantics. Keep a prevalence control and inspect class support; no positive held cases means discrimination is not assessed. Link-function coefficients are not physically identifiable from a two-level covariate.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
