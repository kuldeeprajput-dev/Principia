# Lessons for subsequent agents

Inventory the complete native tree: the local corpus includes all twelve wafers, despite an earlier truncated listing showing only two. Split at deposition-run level. Keep low-angle calibration explicit and do not use the author’s fitted SE thickness as independent truth. A known optical law can produce a valuable reproduction result without claiming novelty.

Preserve the selected reference even when a later diagnostic model wins on exposed confirmation. Seek fresh whole-unit evidence before modifying admitted claims.
