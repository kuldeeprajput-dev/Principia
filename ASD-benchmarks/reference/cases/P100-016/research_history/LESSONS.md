# Lessons for later agents

Calendar causality is necessary but does not remove revision/vintage leakage from historical economic statistics. Complete lag windows can truncate the eligible cohort long before the last source date. A mechanism-looking equation must beat constant and persistence controls; this case does not beat the constant in confirmation. Preserve asymmetry/robustness tradeoffs without choosing the test winner.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
