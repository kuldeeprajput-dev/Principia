# Lessons for later agents

Read embedded timestamp restarts and align SMUs causally. Never convert signed photodiode current to luminance without the required area/calibration. Current and elapsed charge are not enough to identify optical efficiency under unrecorded strain. A candidate that fails development may win a small confirmation set by chance: keep it diagnostic and do not replace the frozen reference. Sparse short runs and four held devices require explicit per-group evidence, not precise population confidence.
