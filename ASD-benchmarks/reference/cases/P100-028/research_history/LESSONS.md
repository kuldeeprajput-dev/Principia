# Lessons for subsequent agents

Separate native measured matrices from numerical inverse-design targets. Preserve complete directedmode matrices when splitting pump conditions. Do not assign dimensionless S-parameter or quantum-fidelity meaning to an unnormalized instrument channel. Small deterministic error differences do not justify a universal mechanism claim.

Preserve the selected reference even when a later diagnostic model wins on exposed confirmation. Seek fresh whole-unit evidence before modifying admitted claims.
