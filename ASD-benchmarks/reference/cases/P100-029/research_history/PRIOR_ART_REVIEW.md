# Prior-art and novelty assessment

Petrenas et al., JACS2025, already demonstrate binding and confinement effects, FRET and anthracene photochemistry. Equilibrium binding, Hill curves and fluorescence quenching are established model families. This experiment tests condition transfer of those families; it does not establish a new binding law.

Primary sources read during this campaign: https://zenodo.org/records/13335904 and https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.4c16633. Source methods and known conclusions informed hypotheses before selection. Targeted title/domain searches were performed on2026-10-01. Search records in `PRIOR_ART.json` identify the primary URLs. This limited targeted review does not establish exhaustive novelty.
