# Lessons for later ASD

Freeze a calibration window before spectral normalization. A future fluorescence maximum is an outcome, not an available predictor. Do not call fluorescence attenuation a uniquely identified binding mechanism.

Four development and two confirmation peptide–dye conditions share peptides and dyes. Spectral means mix intensity and band-shape responses. Fluorescence is not independently measured occupancy; optical controls would be needed to distinguish binding from quenching. Replicates are linked, not counted as independent conditions.

Future revisions must label this confirmation cohort exposed and obtain new evidence before using fresh-confirmation language. Preserve scientific failures; do not relabel a later comparator winner as the original selected model.
