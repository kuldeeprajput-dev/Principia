# Replay

The final package is standalone: `python package/run.py`. Full native reconstruction and integrity checks: `python validate.py /path/to/local-datas`.

Development tables are deterministic native outputs; config and fold models record every fitting choice. To refit, copy this research folder to a disposable location, preserve the old freeze/receipts separately, remove those copied gates, and rerun the recorded baseline/attempt configs with develop.py. This is retrospective replay and cannot be described as fresh confirmation.
