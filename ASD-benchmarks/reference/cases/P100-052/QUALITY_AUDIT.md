# P100-052 current quality audit

Targeted current audit of existing source-bound scientific evidence. It is combined with whole-collection native reconstruction, replay and independent numerical checks; it is not a new discovery attempt.

## Supported scope

Calibrated wake-shape models supply a bounded field reconstruction task.

## Limits and counterevidence

One reserved control and correlated interpolated cells cannot demonstrate an actuation law or independent spatial replicates.



## What would improve the science

Acquire multiple control repeats/tunnel conditions; distinguish raw line-of-sight measurements from interpolated uu products and score turbine-power effects independently.
