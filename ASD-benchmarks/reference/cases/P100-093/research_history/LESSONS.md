# Lessons for later agents

A single FACS plate does not become a large biological cohort because it contains thousands of events. Keep paired controls, wells and all quantiles together. Use explicit native channel names when paper captions and methods disagree; do not invent dye conversion. Distinguish reproducible distribution calibration from transporter mechanism or molecular concentration. Compare scaling with shifts and flexible maps, and retain saturating alternatives as identifiability counterexamples.
