# Lessons for later agents

Use matched robust-estimation controls before attributing gains to equation structure. Separate post-trip distance conditioning from pickup-time forecasting. Keep large errors and unsuccessful route-slope ablations visible. A lower physical-unit error does not turn observational route composition into a universal speed law.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
