# Scientific validation disposition

Supported as a scoped predictive extension; novelty and demonstrated industrial impact are not established.

Original native hashes and final fitted-state freeze are preserved. Confirmation uses no fitting. Owner integrity/replay checks appear in VALIDATION.json; separate reviewers and the shared benchmark engine provide additional checks. Code review is not experimental replication.

MeanTKE2 is author-derived from turbulent measurements; mean wind/thermal gradients are observational predictors, not mechanical interventions. Flag0 subset can select calmer/instrument-quality regimes; no universal turbulence closure.
