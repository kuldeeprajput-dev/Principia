# Source and units

CFPB National Age-Friendly Banking Survey coded CSV; labeled CSV is duplicate representation. BRANCH1/2 defines pastmonth branch use; codes77/98/99 excluded. MOBILE/WEB are independent channel questions with same recall interval; no causal displacement inferred. WEIGHT_FINAL corrects whole banked adult sample; alternate weight pertains to an oversample and is excluded. AGE7 and INCOME9 are ordered categories, not years/dollars. BR_PRESENT/BR_NET_CLOSED are CFPB-linked ZIP-level access/closure indicators. Actual native coding1Yes/2No is verified against the matched labeledCSV, contradicting dictionary0/1; corrected deterministic mapping, not imputation. Missing domains and nonpositive weights excluded deterministically. Cross-validation leaves whole Census regions out; only one reserved region limits generalization and uncertainty.
