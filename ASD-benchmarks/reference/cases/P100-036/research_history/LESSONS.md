# Lessons

Resolve endpoint units before fitting. An undocumented relative-water-content scale and conflicting DAS definitions justify a pre-fit endpoint revision, not fabricated physiology. Join source metadata by exact plantID because row order differs. Hold repeated observations of each plant together. Separate cross-readout prediction from independent assay confirmation: shared fluorescence inputs can predict well without revealing a new molecular law. A small mean gain over treatment/cultivar needs paired plant uncertainty and careful scope.
