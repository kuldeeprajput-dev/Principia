# Lessons

Marginals do not determine a joint response, but using them is a contemporaneous diagnostic, not prospective yield prediction. Enforce Frechet bounds and expose all calibration gates. Biological coediting cannot be identified uniquely from fluorescence association without optical controls and independent genotype assays. One whole held replicate is stronger than random event splits but still does not establish population transfer. A selected simple equation may beat independence and still lose to a flexible comparator; preserve both conclusions.
