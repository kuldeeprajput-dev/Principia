# Lessons for later agents

When a multiplicative law fails at short runtimes, explicitly test a positive additive overhead instead of adding unrelated static covariates. Audit Jacobian rank and near-zero floors; successful simplification can be stronger evidence than extra coefficients. SDK-specific powers and a unit exponent failed development improvement and remain visible. Keep author failures and conditional-success limits separate from positive timing scores. A calibrated runtime surrogate does not establish quantum advantage or industrial scheduling savings.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
