# P100-053 current quality audit

Targeted current audit of existing source-bound scientific evidence. It is combined with whole-collection native reconstruction, replay and independent numerical checks; it is not a new discovery attempt.

## Supported scope

Causal same-hole history supports an industrial torque-forecasting benchmark.

## Limits and counterevidence

Memory equations fail to outperform matched robust/tree controls; torque is angle-mean, not a peak or joint-strength endpoint.



## What would improve the science

Correct endpoint wording in shared diagnostic labels; use independent clamp-force or joint-strength measurements for a separate operational qualification task.
