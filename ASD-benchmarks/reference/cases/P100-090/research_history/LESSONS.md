# Lessons for later agents

Test causal prefixes explicitly when native source velocity is derived. Normalize source units and relative time origins before defining the endpoint. Keep all condition repetitions together and distinguish group-level order prediction from individual behavioral mechanism. Short trajectories require an honest common horizon, not elimination after seeing performance.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
