# Lessons for later agents

Respect labeled native CSVs even when codebooks display numericcodes. State-balanced weighted risk differs from national prevalence. Work-status ablation and freely estimated household equivalence failed in development; retain them. A small numerical improvement is not novelty or a deployable financial screening policy.

Preserve whole-group information budgets and all negative results. Do not reuse this exposed confirmation as fresh evidence after adjusting a rule. Maintain source-native hashes and avoid treating a predictor as a causal mechanism.
