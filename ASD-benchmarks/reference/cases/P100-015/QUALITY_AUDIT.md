# P100-015 current quality assessment

Piecewise resource and age/work associations yield a compact regional-transfer predictor of the reported inability-to-pay response.

**Scope.** Positive-weight complete cases, state-balanced within-state survey-weighted predictive risk; not national prevalence or causal financial access. Income proxy upper bracket175k is a modeling convention. D1A0 reflects no paid work, not unemployment or a job-loss shock.

**Validation unit.** Respondents nested in whole states; source respondents may share unreported household/environmental dependencies. States not independent populations.

**Evidence.** 6 substantive investigations; all current outcomes are exposed. Preserve the development-selected reference even when another frozen model scores better afterward.
