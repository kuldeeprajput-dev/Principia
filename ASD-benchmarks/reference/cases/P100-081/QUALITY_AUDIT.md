# P100-081 current quality assessment

An uncalibrated two-phase carbon-density mixture improves transfer to unseen countries over simple controls.

**Scope.** Texture is unavailable in six countries and excluded rather than imputed. C stocks are excluded because they use bulk density algebraically.; Observational associations do not identify an agroforestry treatment effect. Protocol adaptations and country effects may limit transfer.

**Validation unit.** Entire country/site portfolio, with all transects, fields and depths linked. Eight countries are convenience samples, not a random population sample.

**Evidence.** 5 substantive investigations; all current outcomes are exposed. Preserve the development-selected reference even when another frozen model scores better afterward.
