# Lessons for later ASD

When multiplicative normalized dose laws fail, test whether the remaining response is additive and bounded. Challenge extra genotype coefficients and conservation constraints on development groups; do not interpret a fitted current ceiling as an independently measured vesicle count.

Six reserved animals provide internal experimental-group evidence. Calcium is increased sequentially, so dose and recording time are confounded. Currents also depend on postsynaptic responsiveness and recording stability. The negative early-calibration coefficient is predictive, not a demonstrated causal depletion effect.

Future revisions must label this confirmation cohort exposed and obtain new evidence before using fresh-confirmation language. Preserve scientific failures; do not relabel a later comparator winner as the original selected model.
