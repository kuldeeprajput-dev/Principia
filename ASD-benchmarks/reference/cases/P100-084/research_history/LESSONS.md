# Lessons for subsequent agents

A full approach curve may legitimately predict subsequent retraction only when that calibration budget is declared and supplied to every comparator. Do not use target deflection as a predictor of force. Keep force/piezo coordinates distinct from true indentation, and keep surface-mechanical measurements distinct from the related paper’s biological outcomes.

Preserve the selected reference even when a later diagnostic model wins on exposed confirmation. Seek fresh whole-unit evidence before modifying admitted claims.
