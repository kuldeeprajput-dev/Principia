# Lessons for later ASD

Use matched pre-treatment calibration and retain the originally selected model after final exposure. A multi-exponential fit with vanishing amplitude or nearly equal rates does not identify two physiological compartments.

The paper describes ten antibiotic-trial calves, while this workbook contains seven complete calves. Only two are reserved. There is no untreated parallel group or dose series in this task; treatment, age and calendar time are inseparable. The paper reports a dosing statement that this benchmark does not use as a verified dosing recommendation.

Future revisions must label this confirmation cohort exposed and obtain new evidence before using fresh-confirmation language. Preserve scientific failures; do not relabel a later comparator winner as the original selected model.
