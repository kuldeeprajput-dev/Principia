# Source and units

The source ZIP contains34MATLAB5-format DAT figure tables and three illustrative movies. Figure3A is generated by the authors' social-force simulation and excluded. The paper identifies the remaining external conditions in order and cites Zhang2011 (ref4), Zhang2012(ref62), and Cao2017(ref63). F3D/F3E are reserved together as the potentially linked multidirectional family.

Specific-volume/speed tables have exactly two columns; their axes use m²/person and m/s. The density-speed representations are duplicates and are not separate samples. Speed-flow or step-frequency identities are never independent evidence. Original run/person IDs and exact original archive accessions are absent; this bounds all inference to within-source condition transfer.
