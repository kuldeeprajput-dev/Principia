# Dataset card

Principia-100 is an open, source-aware corpus with 100 explored scientific portfolios: 99 prediction-equipped cases and one source-adequacy abstention. The local registry contains 135 numerical task versions, 1381 model/task comparisons and one nonpredictive assessment. It is not a closed-world list of uniquely correct discoveries.

## Composition and history

The underlying source corpus is version 0.1.1. Its sources span measured, calibrated, author-derived and computational data. Native quality flags and publisher preprocessing are inherited rather than relabeled as raw instrumentation. The first 20 scenarios were used during Principia development. All supplied evaluation outcomes are currently exposed. Original campaign reservation/freshness is retained as historical metadata only.

The local registry has 135 task versions, 130 using the original corpus and five requiring explicit supplementary data. Four supplementary tasks measure PLA storage/loss response from CC BY 4.0 archives. The corrected cycling task uses earlier-stage annotation data whose redistribution basis is not sufficiently established by the repository's software MIT license; its executable data/task is withheld from the public-ready projection. This does not remove the original cycling task.

## Intended use

Compare different equations or predictors on identical measured endpoints under identical information access; inspect reproducible hypotheses, failures and validation designs; develop agent discovery workflows; propose new measured endpoints through an independent task-admission process.

Do not use exposed-target scores as claims of blind generalization, model-training independence, scientific priority, causal mechanism, clinical utility or industrial deployment benefit. These require additional evidence. Source publications and public code may appear in model pretraining.

## Evaluation limits

Many cases have few independent groups or one apparatus, installation, material batch or event. Group-level effects and uncertainty are descriptive within the contract. Temporal transfer at calibrated sites differs from prediction at unseen sites. Initial calibration differs from a truly uncalibrated forecast. Derived physical identities and source-fitted quantities cannot independently verify the same mechanism used to create them.

Reference models preserve useful positive and negative findings. No fundamental new law or demonstrated deployment impact is certified by this standardization. Reviews are advisory agent assessments and not independent laboratory replications or definitive literature adjudication.

## Access, provenance and rights

Native corpus files stay under local-datas or its published scenario equivalent. The adapters verify source hashes before parsing. Original evidence remains intact; the clean layer copies only selected evidence and records its lineage. Public-ready export uses an explicit allowlist and excludes operational/private material and unresolved supplemental data.

Original source terms, attributions and citations remain controlling for source and derived scientific data. Generated benchmark documentation/review and code are separately identified in the per-asset register. Rights of a software repository do not automatically establish rights to every dataset in it. No source acquisition, new paid model runs or publication was performed during this standardization.

## Completion campaign: 2 October 2026

The final 45 cases add 44 numerical tasks, one source-adequacy assessment, 450 model/task comparisons and 114 finding records from 267 substantive investigations. Original 55 scientific portfolios remain preserved. Five investigations concern source adequacy; the medical-imaging subset cannot support a clinical score. Case 5's constant target illustrates why perfect accuracy is not itself discovery.

Current protocols for 2 and 88 correct unavailable information without changing saved predictions. Core point-error arithmetic remains 3.0.0-rc.1; probability annotation 1.1, survey weighting 1.0, source-adequacy 1.0 and scientific diagnostics are explicit extensions. Same-map interpolation, same-device calibration, processed physiological traces, author-reported uncertainties and computational certificates are labeled according to their actual evidential scope.
