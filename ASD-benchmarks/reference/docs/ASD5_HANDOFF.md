# Lessons from the ten-case ASD campaign

1. Audit source semantics before discovery. Byte-identical bioreactor files, a biosensor time column copied as response, reused participants and duplicate representations can manufacture apparent laws.
2. Reserve whole physical units, then test strong simple controls. Persistence and unit-mass recovery defeated every proposed extension in several cases.
3. Freeze selection and stopping before viewing confirmation. Cases 54 and 64 had retrospectively better alternatives; they remain diagnostics and did not replace the chosen reference.
4. Sparse calibration must be explicit. FET reconstruction uses three same-device currents; it is not uncalibrated device prediction. Beam response uses an early loading prefix. Biosensors and optical cells have fixed early windows.
5. Declare measurement timing. Post-test geometry and contemporaneous EG/throughput assays yield conditional response tasks, not prospective control laws.
6. Exposure is a scientific property. Case 85's four evaluation targets were all seen during schema inspection; its evidence is retrospective. All current outcomes are exposed to future agents.
7. Separate prediction from mechanism. Two kinetic channels are not two identified species; fitted apparent toughness is not intrinsic toughness; photodiode current is not luminance; nominal-area scaling is established physics.
8. Test complementary outcomes only with source-defined meaning. Public-goods coordination thresholds come from game rules, and both players are aggregated. Report specificity with F1 and exclude incomplete abstained pairs from event scoring with explicit coverage.
9. Preserve cadence and eligibility limitations. Case 64's fast-calibration/alignment contract excludes slow-cadence runs; two held devices have only 4 and 37 eligible rows. It is a restricted negative benchmark.
10. Keep presentation and execution separate. Every case has a concise two-page reader note, frozen executable states, task contract, source-native adapter, complete attempts and independent reviews. Future agents can contribute different valid equations or propose a new endpoint with a separate reviewed contract.

## Next exploration standard

Use the authoritative instruction.md and versioned registry. Do not reuse exposed confirmation as fresh evidence. New datasets or genuinely new independent groups are needed for new confirmation claims. Do not count retries, seed changes or cosmetic parameter variants as substantive attempts. A supported negative result is a valid outcome; do not manufacture a positive equation or industrial effect.
