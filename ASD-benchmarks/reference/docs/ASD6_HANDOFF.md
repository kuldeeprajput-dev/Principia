# Lessons and next-agent handoff

The current corpus has 55 explored and 45 pending scenarios. The twenty-case campaign completed 120 substantive attempts. This document records reusable scientific practice; it is not permission to relax the validation contract.

1. **Resolve the actual native scenario.** Catalog candidates can have documented substitutions. Case 29 contains peptide barrels and case 30 hBN tribology. Work from verified native provenance, not an earlier candidate title.
2. **Separate target timestamps from information release.** EIA's revised snapshot has observation times, not as-issued forecast vintages. Its correction is retrospective. Drone telemetry with incompatible clocks does not justify invented geometry alignment.
3. **Make calibration an explicit resource.** Low-dose synaptic currents, peptide fluorescence, friction endpoints and reactor prefixes change the task. Compare every model under the same budget. Temporal forecasting and contemporaneous diagnosis need distinct language.
4. **Use whole physical groups.** Devices, animals, reactor runs, cast profiles and architectures stay linked. Two floats and many serial profiles are still two platforms. Fifty-three authorities in one month share weather and are not 53 independent national experiments.
5. **Give strong simple controls a fair chance.** Persistence defeated proposed kinetics in several biological cases. Official forecast residuals, shared learning-curve exponents and established line shapes are necessary controls. Flexible-model tuning belongs inside the training fold.
6. **Treat identifiability as evidence, not aesthetics.** A 20,000-minute relaxation fitted on a 1,500-minute window, lower-bound linewidth intercepts and negative precursor conductance prevent mechanistic claims even when prediction is useful. Parameter bounds and conditioning support practical warnings, not a general structural-identifiability theorem.
7. **Use ablations as scientific tests.** Removing nitrate improved Argo oxygen prediction; this challenges nitrate necessity in this predictor, not ocean remineralization. Dose order confounds calcium recruitment with time, and an effective current ceiling is not a counted vesicle pool.
8. **Verify source-derived quantities independently.** QOBLIB CPU Runtime is Gurobi Work Measure. Two reported auxiliary MIP objectives exceed directly computed graph diameter; unfinished optimization permits this slack. A source label is not an independent certificate.
9. **Freeze every continuation and stopping decision before confirmation.** Later winners in cases 26, 43, 44 and others remain diagnostics. They do not replace development-selected references. Stop after two substantive attempts add no predictive, robustness or supported interpretive value.
10. **Correct contracts by versioning.** The all-case audit found seven inherited timing/calibration/cohort descriptions. Corrected v2 contracts preserve all scientific bytes and scores. Never manufacture a new confirmation by renaming an exposed cohort.
11. **Test evaluator semantics directly.** Signed targets, Boolean values, absent events, exact-probability failures, hierarchical weights, abstentions and partial decision groups need adversarial fixtures. A zero-event undefined metric is different from a failed event predictor with F1=0.
12. **Maintain a readable front door.** Each case has final_results, an English PDF/Markdown note, exact equations, a runnable reference and evaluator instructions. Keep attempts and scientific failures in research_history. Operational copies stay outside the release allowlist.

Execute at least five genuinely different scientific attempts with explicit hypotheses, competing models, ablations and failure records. Continue only while development evidence justifies it. Use separate discovery, computational review and scientific-critical review roles. Supply native reconstruction, freeze/replay receipts, whole-group uncertainty and fair baselines. Demonstrate evaluator compatibility for alternative findings.

All existing outcomes are exposed. Reusing them is retrospective development, never fresh confirmation. Novelty requires broader literature assessment and independent adjudication; industrial impact requires an actual decision/intervention evaluation. This campaign demonstrated neither a universal new physical law nor deployed industrial savings.
