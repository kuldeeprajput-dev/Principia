# Scientific review rubric 3.1

The reference baseline is **implemented by GPT-6 Astra**. Review the submitted claim and exact evidence, not its author/model reputation or resemblance to the baseline equation. All existing outcomes are exposed. This is an advisory evidence rubric, not expert consensus, independent experimentation or a discovery leaderboard.

## Distinct questions

| Axis | Evidence required | Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Numerical validity | Exact executable predictions; correct units, eligible cohort, group weights, coverage, matched controls, error distribution and uncertainty | A single best error with omitted failures or different calibration |
| Scientific explanation | Dimensional consistency, identifiable parameters or explicit nonidentifiability, competing explanations, meaningful limits, discriminating tests or orthogonal responses | Better fit, plausible prose, a bound parameter, an algebraic identity |
| Prior-art differentiation | Specific primary publications/equations, search date and scope, exact difference from closest prior result, counterevidence | Model-generated originality claims; absence of a quick search hit |
| Practical value | Named decision, input availability at that decision, calibration/computation burden, actionable output, realistic limits and measured decision outcome if claimed | Retrospective accuracy alone; hypothetical savings presented as measured impact |

Each axis receives supported, partially_supported, unsupported or not_assessed, with exact evidence and scope. Use additional explicit descriptors: explanation compatible versus experimentally identified; novelty differentiation examined versus not adjudicated; potential application versus measured decision utility. Do not turn unassessed evidence into an unsupported verdict, or reward difficult tasks by relaxing validity requirements.

## Reviewer responsibilities and disagreement

Computational review owns numerical execution and computational replay. Scientific review assesses empirical claim support, mechanisms, prior art and relevance. Computational replay and documentation sufficiency are distinct reviewer scopes. Neither establishes independent experimental replication, which remains unestablished here. Declare each dimension's scope when their meaning differs; do not use different labels to conceal a contradiction about the same proposition.

A not_assessed judgment alone is missing coverage, not an opposing finding. Different declared scopes are complementary. Opposing dispositions or dimension judgments about the same scope require explicit adjudication. Agreement does not automatically establish a discovery. Retain both original reviews, hashes, reviewer IDs, model/human environment when known, rubric version, independence/conflict declarations and decision reasons. Missing historical reviewer-environment records remain unknown.

The 100 current scope-aware records under reviews/adjudicated preserve the original role rationales. They are maintenance adjudications, not newly independent reviews. Case74's real grading conflict is resolved against identified mechanism using its failed transfer and retained controls. Other role distinctions do not create new experimental support.

## Executable scientific checks

Attach claim-specific tests to exact code/evidence. Appropriate examples include a conserved quantity tested against independently measured channels, valid mathematical certificates and counterexamples, limiting/sign behavior, native causal-prefix perturbations, parameter rank/conditioning and predictions of an unfitted orthogonal response. Synthetic dimensional or monotonicity checks establish properties of a program, not that nature follows it. Domain experts may disagree; record that disagreement rather than average it into a number.

## Practical-use contract

State who could use the result, which decision it informs, when every input becomes available, required calibration and compute cost, the predicted action/outcome, scope of failure and evidence for benefit. If the corpus measures no operational intervention, mark deployed utility not_measured. A proposed decision metric needs measured outcomes and independently justified costs or thresholds; arbitrary thresholds are exploratory.

## Priority exemplar lessons

- Grid41: recent and weekly bias correction is useful retrospective prediction; revised releases do not establish as-issued availability or dispatch savings.
- Radiation48: independently measured irradiance and dimensionally explicit physics strengthen interpretation; two calibrated sites do not demonstrate unseen-site transfer.
- Microfracture54: meaningful load prediction must acknowledge post-test geometry and one wafer.
- Semiconductor51: calibrated curve compression can be valuable, but same-device anchors carry cost and do not identify contact physics.
- Training43: disclose mixed architecture-wise gains, early-anchor cost and absence of independent training seeds.
- Rheology58: prediction of an unfitted response, nonidentifiability and preserved failure are stronger scientific practice than hiding the failed recycled-material transfer.

These principles follow the separation of accuracy, interpretability and scientific behavior emphasized by [SRBench++](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12321164/). They do not certify the novelty of any Principia finding.
