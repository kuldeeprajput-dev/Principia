## Exact scientific scope

The local collection has **135 numerical task versions, 1,381 model/task comparisons and 252 finding records**, plus one source-adequacy assessment. There are 130 original-corpus numerical tasks and five supplemental tasks. Seven documentation-only v2 aliases repeat 90 comparisons: these are not new experiments, models or discoveries. The latest campaign adds 44 numerical tasks, 450 comparisons and 114 findings.

The public-ready local projection excludes the optional corrected cycling task whose supplemental data rights remain unresolved: **134 tasks, 1,362 comparisons and 251 finding records**, while retaining all 100 scenarios. Native source datasets remain referenced in the original corpus, without duplication in the scientific results layer.

## What is and is not established

Several scoped predictive extensions survive grouped comparisons, including calibration-dependent reflectivity, compiler runtime, burst count response, battery relaxation, hydrogen permeation, adhesion and processed calcium-trace dynamics. Other candidates fail against simple or flexible controls. The graph source has a constant target, so zero error is scientifically trivial. The imaging subset lacks an admissible predictive endpoint. These outcomes remain explicit; no case was forced to yield a positive law.

No universal new physical law, independent laboratory replication, clinical benefit or deployed industrial savings is certified. Primary-source prior-art records are supplied, but priority remains subject to independent scientific review. Agent reviewers' disagreements are retained. All current outcomes are exposed; subsequent same-cohort scores are retrospective. A later confirmation winner cannot replace a development-selected reference in the original validation claim.

## Corrections and reproducibility limits

Case 2 removes a future-window timestamp and case 88 masks unavailable feedback type; corrected v1.1 protocols preserve all frozen predictions. Case 80's dataset citation, shifted dates and two-protocol scope are corrected. Explicit calibration records include pilots, reflectivity anchors and controls. Probability extension 1.1 labels an unregistered 0.5 threshold as descriptive rather than preregistered. Weighted aggregation, source-adequacy assessment and endpoint checks are separate versioned extensions; existing primary numerical semantics remain evaluator 3.0.0-rc.1. The corpus is version 3.3.0-rc.1. [The tested environment](ASD7_ENVIRONMENT.json) records actual packages.

Finite native-prefix tests do not establish causality of publishers' upstream filtering or every possible future mutation. Prediction-file checks do not establish training independence. Existing missing training-membership records and unresolved supplemental rights remain visible. Preservation checks cover inventoried source/scientific evidence, not every operational cache. The [handoff](../docs/ASD7_HANDOFF.md) explains how future agents should improve the corpus without weakening these standards.
