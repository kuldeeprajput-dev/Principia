# Twenty new ASD portfolios and complete quality review

Principia-100 now contains **55 explored scenarios and 45 pending scenarios**. This local campaign adds **20 cases, 120 substantive attempts, 208 frozen model comparisons, 43 finding records and 40 separate computational/scientific reviews**. Findings include reproductions, bounded extensions, informative failures and metrology limits; they are not 43 new laws.

The additional scenarios use existing verified native assets. Cases 29 and 30 follow the actual collected substitutions—peptide barrels and hBN tribology—not the original acquisition candidate titles. No source acquisition, publication or paid external-model run occurred.

Cases **6, 34, 41, 43 and 48** are useful starting examples of scoped predictive extensions. Cases **47, 94 and 95** add bounded environmental diagnostics. Case **44** demonstrates why source-certificate verification is valuable even when discovery models fail. Reproductions and negative results are retained with the same traceability as positive results.

The results do not establish a universal new physical law or demonstrated industrial impact. A compact predictor may be useful within a measured system while its coefficients remain noncausal, its mechanism nonunique, or its practical savings untested. Independent literature adjudication and new experiments would be needed for stronger claims.

## Scientific process

Every new case has at least five substantively different attempts. Hypotheses, competing equations, development-only fitting, group validation, ablations, falsifying examples, uncertainty and stopping decisions are preserved. Cases with continuing development evidence reached seven, eight or ten attempts. Retries and plotting fixes are not counted.

Confirmation followed recorded code/state/selection freezes and did not guide reselection. Target previews and public-source awareness are disclosed. Whole devices, animals, runs, architectures or chronological blocks remain linked. Small and dependent group collections receive descriptive group evidence, not artificially precise row-based population confidence.

Separate computational and scientific-critical agents reviewed each new case. They checked exact source anchors, equations, numerical evidence and claim limits; disagreements remain recorded. These reviews are not independent experimental replication or human novelty adjudication. All outcomes are now exposed for future users.

## Read, reproduce and submit alternatives

Open a case's final_results/FINDINGS.pdf for its concise English introduction; FINDINGS.md is editable. Exact coefficients and frozen runnable code accompany compact evidence. research_history preserves all attempts and scientific failures. Current task versions are listed in CURRENT_TASKS.json; task cards define inputs, units, timing, calibration, grouping and eligibility.

Use the [shared evaluator](../docs/EVALUATION.md) to assess any alternative equation with the same information budget. A different endpoint requires a separately reviewed contract. Scoring prediction files executes no submitted code; optional replay requires explicit local trust. An abstention or justified negative result is scientifically valid.

Everything remains local. The public-ready projection excludes unresolved supplemental cycling data and its task while retaining the original cycling case. Publication is a separate action.
