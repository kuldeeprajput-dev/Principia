# Quality release readiness — 3.4.0-rc.1

The ASD reference baseline is **implemented by GPT-6 Astra**. This is a local, open, retrospective benchmark candidate for inspection. Nothing was published; no source acquisition, new ASD campaign, human expert adjudication or deployed intervention was performed.

## Acceptance evidence

- All 100 cases have current audit cards, machine-readable eligibility and baseline attribution. Default tiers: 35 comparative core, 58 limited-scope, 7 diagnostic/adequacy. Eligibility is task-level, separate from reference success.
- All 135 historical numerical task versions and 1,381 model/task comparisons reproduce unchanged primary errors under evaluator 3.1. The raw-access overlays preserve the original target cohorts; they are not new scientific experiments.
- The 210 fixture checks across 10 suites pass. They cover contradictory versus complementary reviews, abstention denominators, drift/corruption, malformed submissions, probability units, weighting, intervals/events, raw information declarations and a complete synthetic native-adapter/review/admission/rejection/registration workflow in disposable locations.
- A real router example extracts a new feature from native throughput/packet-size CSV columns, reproduces its saved features and predictions, and scores against the frozen target cohort. It is an interface demonstration, not a new finding or proof of arbitrary-code information independence.
- All 1,685 native/context source assets and 15,796 historical scientific assets remain unchanged. Original task packages, final PDFs/Markdown, research histories and review receipts are preserved.
- Every one of 252 historical finding records now has a current source/equation/evidence context or the case 8 adequacy contract. Nine generic novelty/impact statements are presented as limitations, leaving 243 displayed testable/reference finding records. They are not 243 positive discoveries.
- Scope-aware adjudications distinguish replay, documentation and scientific support. Genuine case 74 disagreement is resolved against identified mechanism; no original review is rewritten. Mechanism, prior-art differentiation and practical utility remain separate judgments.

## Corrections and new interfaces

Review combination detects opposing same-scope dispositions and does not misclassify complementary reviewer responsibilities. Case 44 formulation decisions use the full assigned/eligible instance denominator even when submissions abstain. Brier primary error keeps squared-probability units, while absolute-probability diagnostics use probability units. Primary-error arithmetic is unchanged.

Score receipts verify and bind the engine manifest, exact dependency lock, actual numerical environment, task/cohort, submission and input/target hashes. Engine hash checks detect accidental drift against the installed manifest; they are not digital signatures against a malicious replacement of both code and manifest.

Raw-access protocols allow alternative features within explicitly declared native quantities, timing and calibration. They do not permit arbitrary columns merely because an archive is public. Scoring checks declarations without executing submitted code. Trusted extractor/reference replay is explicit and is not a security sandbox. Training independence cannot be proven from a declaration or a good score.

New endpoints pass intake, native reconstruction and frozen-predictor replay, two bound contract reviews, a distinct maintainer decision and separate registration. Rejection reasons remain recorded. Registration cannot overwrite an existing task or silently change its default cohort. Catalog/rights/release refresh is required before exporting later admitted tasks.

## Remaining limits

All current outcomes are exposed. Novelty remains unadjudicated and industrial impact unmeasured. The corpus includes valuable reproductions and falsifications alongside predictive extensions. There is no aggregate discovery score. Neither authorship by a strong model nor successful replay supplies scientific ground truth.

The 333 historically missing model/fold training-membership records remain unverified; they were not fabricated. Small independent-unit counts, one-system calibration, revised vintages, publisher preprocessing and ambiguous measurement semantics are visible in the per-task eligibility restrictions. Future raw submissions need their own evidence reviews; task eligibility is not automatic submission admission.

The optional case 96 supplemental trajectory task remains local-only because its data-specific redistribution basis is unresolved. The public projection retains all 100 cases, 134 numerical tasks/raw-access overlays, 1,362 model comparisons and 251 historical finding records. Auxiliary PV-weather provenance is explicitly qualified at asset level rather than silently attributed to the deposit license.
