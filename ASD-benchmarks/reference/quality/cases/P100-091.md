## Finding-level conclusions

- **P100-091-F01 (predictive_reference; partially_supported):** A finite-setting rule with same-run throughput correction improves over the regularized source-family queue model on both reserved locations, but fails its improvement claim against the matched flexible control.
- **P100-091-F02 (falsified; supported):** The small development gain from a shared within-setting achieved-throughput slope does not survive both reserved locations.
- **P100-091-F03 (metrology_correction; supported):** Neither offered-minus-achieved traffic nor the fitted queue pole identifies packet loss or physical5G capacity in these data.
