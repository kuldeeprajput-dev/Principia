## Finding-level conclusions

- **P100-058-SCI-01 (reproduction; supported):** Positive Maxwell/WLF spectra predict within-batch storage and unfitted loss response, reproducing established constitutive families.
- **P100-058-SCI-02 (predictive_reference; partially_supported):** A stronger nested Maxwell/WLF implementation improves retrospective storage and orthogonal loss response, without identifying individual modes.
- **P100-058-SCI-03 (validated_extension; supported):** After lower-temperature calibration, WLF storage transfer beat Arrhenius on the preregistered PLA3D850 higher-temperature challenge.
- **P100-058-SCI-04 (validated_extension; supported):** After lower-temperature calibration, WLF storage transfer beat Arrhenius for recycled PLA, while its loss prediction fails.
- **P100-058-SCI-05 (validated_extension; supported):** Unfitted PLA3D850 loss prediction is a useful orthogonal response check under frozen storage calibration.
- **P100-058-SCI-06 (falsified; supported):** Recycled-PLA loss response falsifies general storage-to-loss reliability of the calibrated family.
