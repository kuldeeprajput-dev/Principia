## Endpoint, information and evidence

Recorded completed-trip duration conditional on recorded trip distance

Prediction time: Trip distance is recorded after the journey. Task is retrospective travel-time diagnostics with completed distance, not pickup-time ETA. Pickup time and endpoint categories are known; no fare/payment/duration-derived predictors.

Calibration: Forward training on earlier whole days. Jan–Sepdevelopment,Oct–Decconfirmation. Same city/provider system; no future-day targets enter coefficients. Fixed clock windows, no held tuning.

Independent-unit scope: Whole pickup calendar days; serial weather/traffic/vendor dependence persists. Forward month blocks, not random trips.

Completed-trip distance is an after-trip input; this is not pickup-time ETA.
