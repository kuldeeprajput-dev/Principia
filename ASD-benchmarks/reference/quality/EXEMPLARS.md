# How to use the strongest baseline examples

**implemented by GPT-6 Astra**. These examples illustrate useful scientific practice and scoped numerical findings. They are not certified new laws or measured deployment benefits. All evaluation outcomes are exposed.

| Case | Quantitative contribution | Why it is useful | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| [41: grid demand](cases/P100-041.md) | Recent/weekly residual correction reduces MAE from323.862 to283.872MW;50/53 authority groups improve. | Compact executable correction with explicit historical inputs and matched controls. | Revised snapshots do not establish as-issued forecasts, causal operating behavior or dispatch savings. Authorities share conditions. |
| [48: longwave radiation](cases/P100-048.md) | Stefan–Boltzmann scaling with vapor/cloud/site terms gives9.242 versus13.255W/m²;12/14 site-days improve. | Target comes from an independent irradiance measurement; variables and numerical unit conventions are explicit. | Two calibrated sites and correlated days do not demonstrate a universal radiative law or unseen-site transfer. |
| [54: microfracture](cases/P100-054.md) | Geometry-conditioned force relation gives0.017316 versus0.019077mN;17/22 specimens improve against the flexible control. | A directly measured load and interpretable geometric correction permit numerical falsification. | Some geometry can be post-test; one wafer does not establish prospective safe-load design or intrinsic toughness. |
| [51: semiconductor devices](cases/P100-051.md) | Three-anchor transfer-curve equation gives0.016304 versus0.017657µA;7/9 devices improve. | Explicit calibration budget makes sparse characterization comparable. | Anchors have a measurement cost; one array and a bound-hitting exponent do not identify mobility or contact physics. |
| [43: training dynamics](cases/P100-043.md) | Two early anchors plus size-dependent exponent gives0.005277 versus0.007991nats/token for the shared-exponent control. | Learning-curve extrapolation is executable with an explicit observed prefix. | Only2/5 architectures beat the flexible control individually; no independent seeds, new scaling law or demonstrated compute saving. |
| [58: rheology](cases/P100-058.md) | The same frozen Maxwell/WLF coefficients predict an unfitted loss response; recycled-PLA loss fails with log-MAE1.8604. | Orthogonal response, identifiability limits and preserved failure are strong discovery methodology. | Storage fit does not establish a unique relaxation spectrum or general material transfer. |

The baseline remains the development-selected model even when an exposed-cohort alternative later scores better. Report both. Case31 provides a useful additional warning: better relative log runtime error coexists with worse absolute seconds and a paid-in-compute pilot. Case49 similarly improves mean voltage MAE while losing on RMSE and the worst block. Choosing an industrial objective after seeing those outcomes would require a new, explicitly retrospective task.

A future method can outperform these references or offer a different valid finding. Supply its executable relationship, exact information access, independent-unit scope, parameter uncertainty or nonidentifiability, adverse cases and source-aware prior-art distinction. If a claimed practical benefit is not measured in the corpus, mark it unmeasured.
