## Corrections made during publication audit

Reconstruction against the published source corpus exposed differences from local acquisition folders. Case 5 now resolves to its public folder. Nine cases bind approved public provenance documents rather than their local predecessors. Unused, nonpublic context references in five cases were removed from preparation verification. These changes do not alter measurements, numerical adapters, targets, model states or predictions. All affected tasks were reconstructed again successfully. The initial failures and exact before/after metadata hashes are recorded in [PUBLICATION_ADAPTATIONS.json](PUBLICATION_ADAPTATIONS.json).

Case 48's PDF had missing superscript minus glyphs in the Stefan–Boltzmann constant's explanatory text. Its public Markdown/PDF now uses explicit scientific notation. The scientific equation, executable coefficients and historical reader remain unchanged in the local research collection.

The evaluator retains the audited review-combination correction, case 44 full decision-unit denominator, zero-failure F1 handling, and receipts that bind evaluator implementation, dependency lock and runtime. These checks establish implementation validity within tested contracts, not the correctness of every possible scientific assertion.

## Scientific release boundaries

The default-task tiers are 35 comparative core, 58 limited-scope and 7 diagnostic/adequacy. These describe task eligibility, separately from reference-finding success. Read the [100-case audit](reference/quality/README.md) and [prioritized issues](reference/quality/ISSUES.md) before interpreting a score.

Measured targets and exact mathematical quantities support numerical evaluation. Astra equations are fallible baselines. Mechanism identification, novelty priority, independent replication and industrial intervention benefits require evidence beyond retrospective prediction. Small scoring-group counts, same-apparatus dependence, calibration cost, input timing and uncertain publisher preprocessing are disclosed per task. Existing historical findings are not promoted to new laws by this release.

Current evaluation outcomes are exposed. External agents may compare alternative predictors under the same contract, use a registered raw-data information budget, or propose a reviewed new endpoint. No aggregate discovery score or hidden-test claim is made. Prediction scoring runs no submitted code; optional replay is explicitly trusted local execution.
